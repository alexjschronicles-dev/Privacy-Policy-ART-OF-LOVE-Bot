PRIVACY POLICY

Your privacy and security are important to us. This policy explains what information is collected, how it is used, and how we protect it.

Last Updated: June 12, 2026

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 12, 2026 | Supersedes all previous versions.

Information We Collect

LOVE OF ART BOT collects only the data necessary to provide its moderation, security, and utility features within Discord servers. The bot does not collect personal messages, private channel content, or any data beyond what is explicitly listed below.

User IDs

Discord User IDs are stored to associate configuration, warnings, and moderation actions with individual accounts.

Guild IDs

Server (Guild) IDs are stored to maintain per-server configuration and settings.

Channel IDs

Channel IDs are stored for logging, configuration, and command routing purposes.

Role IDs

Role IDs are processed for permission management and automated role assignment features.

Configuration Data

Server preferences, prefix settings, and feature toggles are stored to preserve your customizations.

Moderation Logs

Warnings, kicks, bans, and timeouts are recorded with timestamps for accountability and review.

Data We Process

Beyond stored data, the bot temporarily processes command inputs, message content for prefix detection, and interaction payloads to execute your requested features. This ephemeral data is not persisted beyond the execution lifecycle unless explicitly required for logging.

How We Use Data

🛡 Moderation

Enforce server rules through automated and manual moderation actions including warnings, kicks, and bans.

🔒 Security

Detect and prevent abuse, spam, and unauthorized access to server administration features.

⚙ Command Processing

Parse and execute user commands, returning appropriate responses based on server configuration.

💾 Configuration Storage

Persist server-specific settings so your preferences remain consistent across bot restarts.

📊 Analytics

Aggregate anonymized usage statistics to improve bot performance and feature development.

📋 Logging

Maintain audit trails of moderation actions and configuration changes for server transparency.

Data Retention

⏳ Temporary Data

Ephemeral processing data (command arguments, interaction payloads) is discarded immediately after execution.

📁 Stored Configuration

Server configurations are retained for the lifetime of bot integration or until manually reset.

📜 Moderation History

Moderation records are retained for 90 days, after which they are automatically purged.

🗑 Deletion Requests

Data deletion requests are processed within 30 days. Backups are purged within 90 days.

Data Security

We implement enterprise-grade security measures to protect your data at rest and in transit.

🔒

Encryption

All data encrypted at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.3).

🛡

Access Control

Role-based access with least-privilege principle enforced.

⚡

Infrastructure

Secure, isolated hosting environment with regular patching.

📋

Audit Logging

All administrative access is logged and reviewed periodically.

System Status: Operational

All systems are secure and running normally. No incidents reported.

Third-Party Services

LOVE OF ART BOT relies on Discord's API for core functionality. We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties for marketing purposes. The bot may integrate with Discord-provided infrastructure services (such as Discord CDN) solely for feature delivery.

User Rights

As a user, you have the following rights regarding your data. To exercise any of these rights, contact us through the channels below.

📄 Request Data

Request a copy of all stored data associated with your User ID.

🗑 Request Deletion

Request complete removal of all stored data associated with your account.

✏️ Request Corrections

Request corrections to any inaccurate or incomplete data we hold.

❓ Privacy Questions

Ask any questions about our privacy practices or data handling procedures.

Data Deletion Requests

To request data deletion, please join our Support Server and submit a ticket, or email us via the contact form on our website. We will verify your identity and process your request within 30 calendar days. Please note that some data may be retained for a short period for backup and legal compliance purposes before full deletion.

Children's Privacy

LOVE OF ART BOT is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age of consent in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately, and we will take steps to delete that information promptly.

Changes To This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated via the bot's announcement channel in the Support Server and an updated "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

Children's Privacy

Protecting Minors

We take child safety and privacy extremely seriously.

📋 Age Requirements

LOVE OF ART BOT complies with all applicable age restrictions. Users must meet Discord's minimum age requirements (13+ or 16+ in certain regions) to use the platform. We do not knowingly collect or process data from users below the legal age of digital consent.

🎮 Discord Requirements

We adhere to Discord's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines regarding minor safety. All moderation and security features are designed with additional safeguards for communities that may include younger users.

🔒 Data Protection

Enhanced privacy controls and data minimization practices are applied to any data potentially related to minors. We maintain strict access controls and retention limits for all user data.

🛡 Safety Measures

Proactive content filtering, anti-harassment tools, and automated safety systems help protect all community members. Server administrators have access to additional safety configuration options.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our privacy and security practices.

We store only essential data required for platform operations: User IDs, Guild IDs, Channel IDs, Role IDs, moderation logs, voice analytics (non-content), server configuration, and security event logs. We do not permanently store message content unless required for moderation or compliance purposes.

LOVE OF ART BOT employs enterprise-grade security including AES-256 encryption, real-time threat detection, DDoS protection, strict access controls, comprehensive audit logging, and 24/7 security monitoring. Our infrastructure is hosted on secure, redundant cloud providers with regular security audits.

Yes, absolutely. You have the right to request permanent deletion of your data at any time. Contact our privacy team via the support server or email, and we will process your request within 24 hours. All data is permanently and irreversibly deleted.

Access to user data is strictly limited to authorized personnel only, with granular role-based access controls. We never sell your data to third parties. Third-party services (Discord API, Cloudflare, hosting providers) interact with data only as necessary for platform operations under strict contractual agreements.

Data is retained only as long as necessary for the purposes it was collected. Retention periods vary by data type and are documented in our detailed retention policies. Upon request or when retention periods expire, data is securely and permanently deleted.

You can reach us through our Discord Support Server at discord.gg/Rk4w7s5J6U, email us at privacy@loveofartbot.com, or use the contact section on this page. Our privacy team typically responds within 24 hours.
